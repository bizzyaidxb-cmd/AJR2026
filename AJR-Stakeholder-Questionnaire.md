# AJR Website & Admin Portal — Stakeholder Questionnaire
*Goal: leave this meeting with every decision needed to build the full website and admin portal.*

---

## 1. Organization & legal
1. What is the legal entity behind AJR (trust / society / Section 8 company / informal collective)? Registration status?
2. Is the fundraiser run under AJR's entity or directly under Veer Naari Shakti Foundation? Who receives the money first?
3. Do we have 80G certification (donor tax benefit)? If yes, who issues receipts — us or the Foundation?
4. Any FCRA implications — will we accept foreign donations, or India-only?
5. Who legally owns the AJR brand, logo, and website?
6. Who signs off on the rider indemnity/waiver, and do we have a lawyer drafting it?

## 2. The event
7. Confirmed ride date — Republic Day 2027? Is it annual-only or multiple rides per year?
8. Final list of launch cities, and the city host/marshal for each?
9. Rider capacity per city? What happens when a city is full (waitlist?)?
10. Is there a registration fee? If yes: amount, what it includes (kit? badge? breakfast?), and is it a donation or a fee (tax treatment differs)?
11. Pillion riders allowed? Minimum age / license requirements?
12. Is the "Proudly Indian" dress theme mandatory or encouraged?

## 3. Money flow & fundraising
13. Where does each rupee go — full split: Foundation / event costs / platform costs?
14. Fundraising target for year one?
15. Payment gateway preference (Razorpay / Cashfree / Instamojo)? Who owns that account?
16. Do riders get personal fundraising pages (collect donations from friends), or is it direct donation only?
17. Donation modes to support: one-time, sponsor-a-rider, club-level, corporate/CSR?
18. Refund policy for registrations?
19. Who reconciles payments, and how often do funds transfer to the Foundation?

## 4. Riders & registration
20. Exact data to collect at registration (name, phone, bike, blood group, emergency contact, T-shirt size…)? Anything legally required?
21. Club/team registration as a unit — yes/no? Does a club captain manage their members?
22. Do riders get anything physical (badge, certificate, kit)? Who fulfils and ships it?
23. Should past riders' profiles/badges carry over year to year (accounts) or is each event standalone?
24. WhatsApp is the default rider channel in India — do we integrate WhatsApp notifications, or email/SMS only?

## 5. Content & brand
25. Sign-off on the locked brand direction (Sindoor & Peacock palette, Special Gothic Condensed One display type — revised from Rozha One, current homepage) — any objections today?
26. Final tagline: "We ride. They live forever." — approved?
27. Who provides martyr family stories, and who obtains written consent from families?
28. Photo/video policy: can we publish rider photos from events? Consent mechanism?
29. Languages: English-only at launch, or Hindi too? Others later?
30. Who is the single content approver (one name, not a committee)?

## 6. Website scope & timeline
31. Hard launch date for the website? Work backwards: registrations should open how many weeks before ride day?
32. Phase 1 must-haves vs can-wait — confirm: Home, About/Cause, Rides listing, Registration + payment, Donate. Agree?
33. Leaderboards, gallery, rider dashboards — Phase 2 acceptable?
34. Domain name — do we own it already (amarjawanriders.in / .com)? Who holds the registrar account?
35. Budget range for build + first-year running costs (hosting, gateway fees, tools)?
36. Who maintains the site after launch — volunteer, agency, or hire?

## 7. Admin portal
37. Who are the admin users? Roles needed: super admin / city host / finance / content editor — which of these, and roughly how many people each?
38. What must city hosts manage themselves: their ride details, capacity, rider check-ins, local sponsors?
39. Ride-day operations: do we need on-ground check-in (QR scan of rider badge) or is a printed list fine for year one?
40. Finance views: what reports do stakeholders need (donations by city, by day, pending transfers, 80G receipt log)? Export to Excel required?
41. Content management: should admins edit pages/stories/gallery themselves (CMS) or is developer-updated acceptable for year one?
42. Communications: send announcements to riders from the portal (email/WhatsApp blast)? Segmented by city?
43. Approval workflows: does a rider registration or a family story need approval before going live?
44. Data & privacy: who is accountable for rider personal data? Retention policy? (India's DPDP Act applies.)
45. Audit needs: do stakeholders/Foundation need read-only access to verify funds raised?

## 8. Marketing & partners
46. Sponsors confirmed or in pipeline? Do sponsors need logo placement, a page, or ride-level sponsorship packages?
47. Influencer/club outreach plan — does the website need a "register your club" funnel from day one?
48. Official social handles — created and owned by whom?
49. Press kit needed on the site at launch?

## 9. Success & risk
50. What does success on ride day + 1 look like — riders? ₹ raised? cities completed incident-free?
51. Biggest risk in stakeholders' view: safety incident, low turnout, payment/trust issues, or political sensitivity? What's the mitigation for the top one?
52. Safety plan: marshals, police permissions per city, insurance — whose responsibility, and does the website need to surface any of it (safety FAQ, permissions status)?
