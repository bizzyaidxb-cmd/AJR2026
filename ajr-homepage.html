Why we ride

For the families of the brave

Every rupee raised goes to the Veer Naari Shakti Resettlement Foundation, supporting the widows, children and dependents of India's martyred soldiers with a dignified, sustainable livelihood.

One day. One ride. A salute that never fades.

The experience

One day like no other

Three experiences woven into every AJR ride — joy, brotherhood and national pride.

The ride

A guided convoy through your city — pre-defined route, trained marshals, and the roar of a thousand engines in tribute.

The brotherhood

Dress to the theme — Proudly Indian. Meet the nicest people on motorcycles, from vintage clubs to weekend riders.

The honour

Every registered rider carries the AJR badge — a mark of the salute you rode, and the family your miles support.
