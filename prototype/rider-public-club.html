Why I ride

I started Spice Coast Riders in 2019 with four bikes and a WhatsApp group. Now we're 42 riders, and every Republic Day we ride Kochi together for the families of India's martyred soldiers.

Back me and it counts twice — for my ride and for my club's total. Every rupee reaches the Veer Naari Shakti Foundation.
