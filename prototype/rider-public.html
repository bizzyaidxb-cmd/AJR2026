Why I ride

My grandfather served 24 years in the Madras Regiment. Every Republic Day I ride so that the families of the men who didn't come home know that we remember — and so that the Veer Naari Shakti Foundation can keep standing behind them.

Every rupee you give here goes to those families. Not to me, not to the ride. To them.
